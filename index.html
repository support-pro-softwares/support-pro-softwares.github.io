Tidy Labels for Confluence

Tidy Labels for Confluence lets Confluence admins and space admins rename, merge and delete labels across a whole site or one space, and find near-duplicate and rarely used labels to clean up. Every change shows a preview first and runs with your own Confluence permissions, and you can undo what a job changed.
